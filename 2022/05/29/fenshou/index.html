说好不分手We Promised Not to Break Up说好不分手

前提摘要

没想到一晃距离上次更新这个小站已经过去两年了，上次更新的时候我还是大学生，现在已经毕业工作好久了。感觉陆陆续续读了不少的书，但都没怎么记录，很多读完也不太记得了。或者我也没有太多的冲动去记录或者分享下自己所学习到的，当然也跟我读书时间变少有关，感觉去年在香港一直浑浑噩噩的，不是在倒卖吉他就是在赶工，但还是一篇论文都没能发表，主要原因肯定还是我自己工作效率不高，然后也是因为做的方向感觉不是特别容易能发表的，进度比较缓慢，到现在也没有把之前的任务再完成。而最近有了工作以后，感觉更不想做不感兴趣的学术了，工业界也许因为我在的公司就我一个中国人，所以真的是活少钱多，而且远程工作也是我理想的生活，唯一就是疫情原因困在乡下，但也挺好，每天生活作息都很规律，没什么花销，赚的钱还完贷款后都能存下来。当然我其实也可以去全世界任何地方，只要每天还能参加standup报告这样。Anyways，我还是有准备考试，毕竟我觉得我做学术的沉默成本不低，然后我也有一些想法能做一些有意义的工作。当然也需要更多的练习，可能还是再工作一段时间看看。

之前的感情

距离我上一段正式的感情结束可能有两三年的时间了吧，也是我第一次生理和心理上真正体会到喜欢/爱一个人的感觉，当然我也没做太出格的事情，只是因为对方有男朋友或者说有更好的选择而拒绝了我，当然我也不知道我们能不能算交往，只是每天一起吃饭，一起学习，一起看电影，可能也只是个学伴。不过都过去挺久的了，虽然我是到最近才真正的走出来（我真的比较慢热），看到她又跟我发男朋友的情头就彻底放下了。其实之前在她主动找我聊天的时候还有一些幻想，但很快就被现实击溃了。不过她还是对我产生了一些深远的影响，比如是促使我又拾起吉他的直接原因，然后我也有很多反思，1）尊重对方的选择。我记得有一个moment是我们走在路上，她突然蹲下问我“我们该怎么办？你是怎么考虑的”我当时就懵了，我一直都没有什么临场应变的能力，所以当时唯一能做的或者说想到的就是先扶她起来，然后也不知道说什么，但如果是现在的我，应该会说“我尊重你的选择”，不管怎么样选择都是每个人自己做并且需要为之承担责任的，就像我当时的承诺可以为了她放弃出国也是真心的，但是错过了那个时机之后就的确不太能做出相同的承诺，所以我后来都会去世界上不同的地方去认识不一样的朋友。当然，像感情这种双向选择的事情，选择权不仅仅在我手里，更是在对方手里，我也不能越俎代庖帮别人做决定，不合适其实也没什么，好聚好散，也不需要一味讨好/打扰别人。2）有礼有节，耐心倾听。我一般都是会回礼的，滴水之恩当以涌泉相报，当然送礼这个事情跟“恩情“也不太一样，但我觉得还是可以适当的回赠礼物的 3）照顾别人之前，先做好自己，有时候一味的付出也只能感动自己。 4）想做就做别拖别等别后悔

本书的学习

虽然我自己有一些反思，但我在感情上的确是稚嫩的，并不是很懂得如何与异性相处，所幸在互联网时代很多智慧/经验都在网上能找到，以前我比较习惯问quora，现在中文的话可以看看知乎，虽然我也明白别人的经验不一定能套用在自己身上，但有些问题或者做法别人的经历或者经验的确能给我很多启发。然后我就看到了知乎id 九颜 的大哥，他的很多回答让我觉得很有启发，至少他的说法是符合我的认知的。然后看到他有出一本书叫《说好不分手——如何体面地挽回一段感情》就买回来看了。书不厚很快就看完了。

  • ”爱的基础是尊重，一旦失去了尊重，感情肯定会失衡“

分手初期该怎么做：

  1. 首先，给自己找点事情做
  2. 其次，给自己找一些心灵寄托
  3. 最后，清除掉哪些能勾起回忆的“心锚”

总结

虽然读完了，但我感觉两性问题很多的确读过就是读过，因为我也没有太多实践的经历，所以就有个大概的了解，以后遇到可能知道怎么处理，但感觉更多还是找到合适的人，我原本相信命中注定，但现在觉得更多还是相处，门当户对？当然我也不一定继续在国内卷。每个人的可塑造性都还是很强的，特别是像我这样子的，特别容易适应。ISFJ（by MBTI test)， well, I am still trying to figure out why is MBTI solid.

Previously

I can’t believe it’s been two years since I last updated this little site. Back then I was still a college student; now I’ve graduated and been working for quite a while. I feel like I’ve read a fair number of books here and there, but I barely recorded any of them, and I don’t remember much of a lot of them. Or maybe I just haven’t felt much urge to record or share what I’ve learned. Of course, that’s also because I spend less time reading now. Last year in Hong Kong I was kind of drifting through the days, either flipping guitars or rushing to meet deadlines, and still didn’t manage to publish a single paper. The main reason is surely my own low efficiency, but also the direction I was working on didn’t seem easy to publish in, so progress was slow, and to this day I still haven’t finished the tasks from back then. And now that I have a job, I feel even less like doing academic work I’m not interested in. In industry, maybe because I’m the only Chinese person at my company, it really is little work for a lot of money, and remote work is my ideal way of living. The only thing is that I’m stuck in the countryside because of the pandemic, but that’s actually pretty nice too: my daily routine is very regular, I barely spend anything, and after paying off my loans I can save whatever I earn. Of course, I could actually go anywhere in the world, as long as I can still attend the daily standup. Anyways, I’m still preparing for exams, since I think the sunk cost of my academic path isn’t low, and I also have some ideas for doing meaningful work. Of course I need more practice too; maybe I’ll work for a while longer and see.

My previous relationship

It’s probably been two or three years since my last real relationship ended. It was also the first time I truly felt, physically and emotionally, what it’s like to like/love someone. I didn’t do anything too out of line; she turned me down because she had a boyfriend, or you could say a better option. I don’t even know if what we had counts as dating. We just ate together every day, studied together, watched movies together; maybe I was only a study buddy. But it was a long time ago. Although I only truly got over it recently (I really am slow to warm up and slow to let go), seeing her send me a couples’ profile picture with her boyfriend made me let go completely. Before that, when she reached out to chat with me on her own, I still had some fantasies, but they were quickly crushed by reality. Still, she had some lasting effects on me. For example, she was the direct reason I picked up the guitar again. I also did a lot of reflecting: 1) Respect the other person’s choice. I remember one moment when we were walking down the street and she suddenly squatted down and asked me, “What should we do? What are you thinking?” I just froze. I’ve never been good at thinking on my feet, so the only thing I could do, or could think of, was help her up, and I didn’t know what to say. But the me of today would probably say, “I respect your choice.” No matter what, choices are something each person makes for themselves and has to take responsibility for. Just like back then, my promise that I could give up going abroad for her was sincere, but once that moment passed I couldn’t really make the same promise again, which is why later I went to different places around the world to meet different kinds of friends. Of course, in something like a relationship, where both sides choose, the choice isn’t only in my hands; it’s even more in the other person’s hands. I can’t overstep and make decisions for someone else. If it’s not a fit, that’s fine; part on good terms, and there’s no need to keep trying to please or bother the other person. 2) Be courteous and appropriate, and listen patiently. I usually return the favor when someone gives me something. As the saying goes, repay a drop of kindness with a gushing spring. Of course, giving gifts isn’t quite the same as “kindness,” but I think it’s good to return gifts when appropriate. 3) Before taking care of others, take care of yourself first. Sometimes giving and giving only ends up moving yourself. 4) If you want to do something, do it. Don’t put it off, don’t wait, don’t regret it.

What I learned from this book

Even though I’ve done some reflecting, I’m honestly still naive when it comes to relationships and don’t really know how to get along with the opposite sex. Luckily, in the internet age a lot of wisdom and experience can be found online. I used to mostly ask on Quora; now for Chinese content I can check Zhihu. I know other people’s experiences don’t necessarily apply to me, but for some problems or approaches, their stories and experience really do give me a lot of inspiration. Then I came across the Zhihu user Jiu Yan. Many of his answers felt really insightful to me; at least what he said matched my own understanding. Then I saw he had published a book called We Promised Not to Break Up: How to Win Back a Relationship with Dignity, so I bought it and read it. The book isn’t thick, so I finished it quickly.

  • “The foundation of love is respect. Once respect is lost, the relationship will definitely become unbalanced.”

What to do in the early stage of a breakup:

  1. First, find something to keep yourself busy
  2. Second, find something for your heart to lean on
  3. Finally, clear out the “emotional anchors” that can bring back memories

Summary

Although I finished it, I feel that with a lot of relationship issues, reading about them is just reading about them, because I don’t have much hands-on experience. So I have a rough idea now, and if I run into these situations later I might know how to handle them, but I feel it’s more about finding the right person. I used to believe in fate, but now I think it’s more about how you get along. Being a good match in background? Of course, I might not keep grinding it out in China anyway. Everyone is still very malleable, especially someone like me who adapts really easily. ISFJ (by MBTI test), well, I am still trying to figure out why is MBTI solid.
