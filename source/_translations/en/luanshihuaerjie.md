title: "Wall Street in Turbulent Times"
---

A really good book that describes what the author saw, heard, and felt. Here's a summary of what I learned:
1. Watch market liquidity; in turbulent times, liquidity can be the straw that breaks the camel's back.
2. Value relationships (sales channels); in good times you need to keep good sales channels and find your own position in how the profits get divided. (from Goldman Saches: If we serve our clients well, our own success will follow)
3. A "new game" is the easiest way to make money (so look for new games, like bitcoin)
4. Don't invest in the stock market (that's where you most easily get fleeced, and who bears the cost in turbulent times) "According to the efficient market theory, the movements of a mature stock market simply can't be predicted"
