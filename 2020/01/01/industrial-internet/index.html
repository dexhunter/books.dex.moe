I read two books on the topic, one from General Electronics Industrial Internet: Pushing the Boundaries of Minds and Machines and the other Industrial Internet: Frame and Technology. I am interested because I am at Industrial Internet Key Laboratory at Zhejiang University. And here is my brief review.

GE’s Industrial Internet: Pushing the Boundaries of Minds and Machines mainly covers these applications of the industrial internet: commercial aviation, rail transport, power generation, oil and gas development and distribution, and healthcare. The three key elements of the industrial internet: intelligent devices, intelligent systems, and intelligent decision-making.

Intelligent systems include:

  • Network optimization
  • Maintenance optimization
  • System recovery
  • Learning

The second book, Industrial Internet: Frame and Technology, says an industrial internet system has four parts:

  • Sensing and identification layer
    • Object identification: QR codes, RFID
    • Object state
    • Scene recording technology
    • Positioning technology: GPS satellites, BeiDou satellites, wifi positioning, radio-frequency identification positioning
  • Network connection layer
    • Internet access
    • Network connectivity
  • Platform aggregation layer: cloud computing
  • Data analysis layer
    • Prediction
    • Analysis
    • Feedback

The Internet of Things belongs to the sensing and identification layer of the industrial internet, and covers a wider range of smart objects than the industrial internet connects.
